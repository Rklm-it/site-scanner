## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end climate engineering, structural precision, and architectural luxury. Tailored for residential estates, premium corporate headquarters, and commercial projects across Kaluga, it repudiates retail air conditioning tropes—such as illustrated snowflakes, swirling wind icons, or generic saturated blues. Instead, the interface treats thermal comfort as a discipline of precision engineering, acoustic silence, and flawless integration.

The visual style blends **Dark Luxury Minimalism** with **Industrial Glassmorphism**:
- **Monolithic Deep Fields:** Deep obsidian, cold graphite, and midnight steel foundations construct an expansive, cinematic environment.
- **Architectural Glass:** Precision frosted layers with ultra-fine, hairline perimeter strokes emulate high-spec architectural glazing and technical instrumentation panels.
- **Atmospheric Luminosity:** Laser-focused arctic cyan and pure silver accents cut through darkness, conveying calibrated airflow, sub-zero precision, and clinical cleanliness without decorative clutter.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, silent, disciplined, and uncompromisingly high-performance.

The palette is engineered around pure atmospheric depth, metallic silvers, and disciplined photonic accents.

## Typography

Typography relies exclusively on **Manrope**, selected for its architectural geometric construction, razor-sharp technical figures, and authoritative neutrality. 

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Rigor:** All thermodynamic measurements, capacity figures (e.g., `BTU/h`, `kW`, `dB(A)`), and prices use tabular numerals with tight letter spacing for industrial alignment.
- **Hierarchy & Tone:** Headlines command attention through heavy optical weight (`800` / `700`) paired with negative tracking, while metadata tags employ `label-caps` in all uppercase with generous positive tracking (`0.12em`) to echo mechanical serial plates.
- **Contrast Discipline:** Pure white (`#F8FAFC`) is strictly confined to primary headings and vital real-time readings. Continuous prose utilizes `#94A3B8` and `#E2E8F0` to prevent screen glare and eye fatigue within dark viewing environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors high-precision structural blueprints: geometric discipline, strict alignment, and intentional expanses of empty field.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column dynamic fluid grid set within a maximum constraint of `1360px`. Gutters sit firmly at `32px` (`2rem`), with page margins scaling symmetrically from `48px` to `80px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `24px` gutters and `32px` outer padding. Product comparisons reflow to horizontal cards with locked telemetry columns.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column grid with `16px` gutters and `16px` margins. Dense technical data toggles into collapsible accordion panels or side-scrolling spec matrices.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Internal card padding defaults to `24px` (`space-lg`) on mobile and `32px` (`space-xl`) on desktop to maintain visual weight and high-end breathing room.
- Sections are delineated by wide architectural rhythm (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`), using ultra-thin horizontal divider rules (`border-structural`) rather than arbitrary vertical whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft, realistic drop shadows. Instead, depth is achieved through **optical filtration, glass stacking, and edge illumination**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Field Layer (`#0A0D12`):** Base structural canvas. Passive, zero elevation.
2. **Plinth Layer (`#11161F`):** Architectural zones, technical calculators, parameter sidebars. Set flush with a 1px top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Glassmorphic Surface (`#161D27` with `rgba(22, 29, 39, 0.72)`):** Primary interactive cards and spec displays. Utilizes `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with a crisp `1px border: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Active Flight Layer:** Modals, engineering blueprints, drop-down telemetry menus. Styled with `rgba(30, 38, 51, 0.85)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, and an ambient back-projected cyan fringe: `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 30px -5px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`.

### Border Radiance
Active cards and interactive components apply an directional edge highlight:
- Default state: Consistent 360° hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Hover state: Border shifts dynamically to a subtle gradient transitioning from `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.5)` at the top-left light source down to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` at the bottom-right.

## Shapes

The geometric approach balances industrial chassis design with ergonomic refinement. Radii are intentional, restrained, and structural.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-badges, technical pill meters, input toggles, and status markers.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for product showcases, engineering spec tables, installation cards, and diagnostic modules.
- **Modal & Container Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Reserved for primary hero panels, full configurators, and floating viewport dialogs.
- **Rule on Form:** Pure circles are permitted solely for hardware status LEDs, radial airflow angle gauges, and acoustic decibel meters. Circular buttons and extreme pill buttons are strictly avoided to maintain an architectural, engineered silhouette.

## Components

### 1. Action Buttons
- **Primary Technical Action:** Deep graphite body (`#11161F`) framed by a luminous `1px` stroke of `#38BDF8`, inner text `#F8FAFC`, with a concentrated frost-cyan drop shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`). On hover, background shifts to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)` with amplified border brightness.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#E2E8F0`. On hover, border brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` and background tints to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- **Text Link / Micro Action:** Manrope bold with `label-caps` tracking, accompanied by an engineered arrow glyph (`→`) that translates `+4px` on hover. Accent color: `#38BDF8`.

### 2. Specification Chips & Engineering Badges
- **Technical Tag:** Compact `4px` radius, background `rgba(22, 29, 39, 0.9)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Text in `label-caps`, color `#94A3B8`.
- **Certified Inverter Status Badge:** Features an active pulsating 6px dot (`#38BDF8`) with an outer box-shadow ring (`0 0 8px #38BDF8`), followed by uppercase monochrome type: `CERTIFIED INSTALLATION • SINCE 2004`.

### 3. Cards (Climate Units & Solutions)
- Built on `bg-surface` (`#161D27`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Visual headers display hardware units silhouetted against dark graphite gradients.
- Micro-metric strip integrated directly into card footers: three equal columns displaying Cooling Power (`kW`), Noise Index (`dB`), and Energy Class (`A+++`), delineated by `1px` vertical rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).

### 4. Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat obsidian fill (`#0A0D12`), height `48px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Typography is crisp `15px` `#F8FAFC`.
- Focus State: Instant edge snap to `#38BDF8` with a subtle interior cyan glow (`inset 0 0 0 1px #38BDF8`). Placeholder text rests at `#475569`.
- Measurement input adornments (e.g., `м²`, `BTU`, `м³/ч`) are anchored on the right edge in uppercase `label-mono-metric` `#64748B`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Checkboxes:** Squared `4px` radius, `#0A0D12` fill, `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. When checked: `#38BDF8` border with a centered cyan geometric checkmark.
- **Architectural Toggle:** Linear rectangular track (`42px × 24px`), dark graphite well, containing a sliding squared block thumb (`18px × 18px`) that transitions smoothly. Active state illuminates thumb in `#38BDF8` with faint surface halo.

### 6. Specialized Components: Precision Climate Selector
- **Area & Thermal Load Slider:** Ultra-fine `2px` track with `#1E2633` background, active segment filled with `#38BDF8`. Thumb is a precision vertical reticle with a live floating readout window indicating square meters and calculated chilling output requirement (`kW`).
- **Acoustic Noise Gauge:** Logarithmic vertical meter showcasing decibel ratings (`19 dB(A) Whispering silence` to `45 dB(A) Standard`) using segmented hairline bars. Nominal quiet zones render in cool cyan, transitioning to warm amber only at commercial load tiers.
